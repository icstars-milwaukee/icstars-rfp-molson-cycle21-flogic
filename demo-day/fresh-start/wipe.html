Flogic fresh start

This page clears what the Flogic app remembers in this browser: the people who signed in here (their PIN copies) and the drive folder that was picked. It never touches your files, the keyring, key files or data.

It works because a page opened from your disk (file://) shares its storage with DataPage/flogic.html in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers. In Firefox or Safari it can't see the app's storage: use the console line in the README instead.
