The problem

  • Ticket exports are big spreadsheets, and the answers are buried in them.
  • The real rows are sensitive, so they can't go into a cloud tool.
  • Staff need quick answers. IT needs control over who sees what.

What Flogic does

Opens your file

Excel (.xlsx) and SQL dumps (.sql), read right in the page.

Shows it at a glance

A dashboard is the first thing you see after a file opens.

Checks it

Questions about the file, each with a rule for passing.

Answers questions

A small database of its own, inside the page, for queries.

One page, three stations

Sheet  ·  Dashboard  ·  Ticket

One view at a time, on a carousel. Open a row and the Details drawer has the whole ticket.

Keep it on tap

Local-first, by design

  • The app is just files. It runs in your browser or as a small desktop app.
  • The server holds no data, no keys, no passwords.
  • Sign in with a key file from IT, kept in your own OneDrive or Google Drive.
  • The rules are built in. Nobody can change them from a screen.
This is where the bridge fills with beer. Say it's "on tap": available when you need it, never poured out.
